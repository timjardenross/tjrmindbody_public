Assessment

A gentle place to begin

This page gives you a calm overview of the assessment path before you step into the app shell. It is designed to feel welcoming, not heavy, so you can decide whether now is the right time to explore more.

What it does

Helps you notice where your capacity sits today and which REVS stage feels most appropriate to begin with.

How it feels

Gentle, low-load, and practical. No pressure, no label, no rush to make a big decision.

What happens next

If it feels right, you can step into the app shell and try the guided assessment flow inside the REVS experience.

Try the portal

The REVS app shell is where the deeper assessment experience lives. This gateway keeps the public page simple while still making it easy to move into the full experience when you are ready.

Inside the shell, you can explore the framework, move through the assessment, and see the stage and recommendations in a more guided format.

If you are just orienting yourself, the Foundation page is the best next stop. If you want to begin, step through the portal.

What the portal is for

  • A calm entry point into the REVS app shell
  • A place to decide whether the assessment feels right for you
  • A bridge into the deeper guided experience, not the full experience itself
  • A better fit for people who want context before they begin

The goal is clarity and ease, with no pressure to move faster than feels right.
