Why this exists

A place to start with capacity

I've spent my career helping organisations become more resilient. Now my mission is to help people do the same, one practical step at a time.

TJR Mind & Body brings together Tim's professional background in operational resilience with the personal reality of rebuilding through chronic pain, surgery, anxiety, setbacks, and demanding seasons of life.

The aim is simple: help people build steadier systems for real life, without hype, shame, or all-or-nothing pressure.

The REVS journey

Four stages that help you move from understanding what is happening to changing the conditions around it.

Recognise

Build language for what is draining capacity and understand the pattern you are living inside.

Regulate

Lower load, restore steadiness, and build a practical regulation toolkit that actually fits your system.

Rebuild

Expand capacity slowly and sustainably by combining pacing, support, and repeatable routines.

Redesign

Change the conditions around you so work, relationships, and commitments fit your actual capacity.
