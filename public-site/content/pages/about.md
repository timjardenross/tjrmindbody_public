## Why TJR Mind & Body exists

For more than 15 years, I have worked in operational resilience, business continuity, crisis management, and major incident response across some of Australia's most complex organisations.

My job has been to help people and organisations prepare for uncertainty, respond under pressure, and recover when things don't go to plan. In that setting, resilience is about practical planning, clear decisions, and looking after what matters most.

> My background is in operational resilience. My current work is coaching and educational support for people who want a clearer way to think about capacity, pressure, and what comes next.

At the same time, I have been living a very different resilience story.

For more than 20 years, I have lived with chronic spinal pain, multiple surgeries, anxiety, depression, burnout, and the ongoing challenge of keeping life moving while also managing my health and a demanding career.

Those experiences changed how I think about resilience.

I learned that resilience is not about pushing harder, ignoring pain, or simply "being stronger." It is about understanding your capacity, noticing your limits without being defined by them, and building practical systems that can make day-to-day life feel more manageable.

There was never a single breakthrough.

There was no miracle treatment or overnight transformation.

Instead, progress came through thousands of small decisions: learning what helped, letting go of what didn't, rebuilding after setbacks, and accepting that change is rarely a straight line.

That journey became the foundation of TJR Mind & Body.

This is where work experience meets lived experience.

It brings together coaching, educational resources, lived experience, and systems thinking to help people make sense of what is going on and work out the next sensible step.

Because resilience is not just for big emergencies.

It is part of ordinary life.

---

## Who this is for

TJR Mind & Body is for people who feel like life has become heavier than it used to be.

You might be living with chronic pain, burnout, anxiety, overwhelming stress, neurodivergence, major life changes, or simply the quiet exhaustion that comes from trying to keep everything together while your capacity continues to shrink.

You do not need to be at rock bottom.

You just need somewhere to start from.

This is not about becoming the most productive version of yourself.

It is about building a life and routine that are easier to live with in real life.

---

## What makes this different

Most resilience advice assumes everyone has the same amount of energy, time, and capacity.

Real life does not work that way.

Your capacity changes from day to day. Health changes. Work changes. Family changes. Unexpected events happen.

Instead of fighting that reality, TJR Mind & Body offers coaching and educational support that helps you work with it.

Everything is built around practical strategies that can help you:

- Understand your current capacity.
- Reduce unnecessary pressure.
- Build habits that are more sustainable.
- Recover more effectively.
- Make clearer decisions during difficult periods.
- Create personal systems that are easier to keep using when life becomes challenging.

The goal is not perfection.

The goal is to help you keep going without making everything harder than it needs to be.

---

## What shapes the approach

The work draws from four connected perspectives:

- **Operational resilience**: understanding how people and systems prepare for, respond to, and recover from disruption.
- **Lived experience**: more than two decades navigating chronic pain, mental health challenges, recovery, and rebuilding.
- **Educational coaching**: turning research and practical thinking into plain tools people can actually use.
- **Systems thinking**: recognising that lasting change comes from improving the environment, routines, and decisions around you, not simply relying on willpower.

## A simple belief

You do not need to become someone else to build resilience.

You need to better understand your capacity, work with it instead of against it, and build systems that support the life you want to live.

That is what TJR Mind & Body is here to help you do.
